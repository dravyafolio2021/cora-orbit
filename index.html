    Everything orbits the picture.

    Orbit keeps the video in charge. Controls sit on glass that lets the scene show through, the progress you make circles the play button, and the things people normally dig through menus for are one tap, one gesture or one sentence away.

    The orbit

    One circle carries the story: the play button wears your progress, and a practice loop fills a ring with every pass. Chapters split the timeline, and the chapter you’re in is always named.

    Lazy by design

    Double-tap the sides to skip, hold anywhere for 2× and slide to tune it, swipe in fullscreen for volume and brightness. Every gesture has a button and a key, so nobody is left out.

    Just ask

    Press ✦ or / and say “too fast”, “loop this”, “skip to the robots” or “sleep after this”. Orbit understands plain words on the device, no account and no server.
