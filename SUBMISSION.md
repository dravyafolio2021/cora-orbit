## Name
Cora ORBIT

## Short description (paste into the form)

Everything orbits the picture. Orbit keeps the video in charge: controls sit on one glass dock that lets the scene show through and thickens on bright frames, and a single circle carries the story. The play button wears your progress as a ring, and a practice loop fills its own ring with every pass. Then it makes the player lazy in the best way: double-tap to skip, hold anywhere for 2× and slide to tune, swipe in fullscreen for volume and brightness, or press ✦ and just say "too fast and I'm going to bed". Orbit understands plain words in the browser, with no server. It also adds what v10 doesn't ship yet: a chapter menu, caption styling, saved preferences and A–B practice loops. Every gesture has a button and a key, focus is always visible, and motion calms down on request.
